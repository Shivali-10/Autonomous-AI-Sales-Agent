Fortivex AI Private Limited (headquartered in Bengaluru, India) builds enterprise Artificial Intelligence, Voice Calling Agents, Cloud Infrastructure, and Cybersecurity automation products for growing businesses and enterprises.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
**Q: Does Fortivex AI support Hindi and Indian regional languages?**
Yes. Growth and Enterprise plans support English, Hindi, Hinglish, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Marathi, Bengali, plus major global languages like Spanish, French, and German.

**Q: How does the AI avoid making up product prices or policies?**
Fortivex uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with strict grounding rules. Every product claim, price, and policy is retrieved from the verified company knowledge base before the LLM generates a response.

**Q: Can I book a live demo with a human solutions engineer?**
Yes! Demo consultations are available Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM IST. We only need your name, contact email or phone number, and preferred date and time.

**Q: How is customer data secured?**
All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Enterprise customers can opt for zero-retention LLM APIs, PII redaction, and single-tenant VPC hosting in AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1).
